Live agent org chart

An example of how Jason uses his agents today. Hermes and Grok Bot are peer interfaces into the same secure context layer: the Obsidian vault as source of truth, GBrain as search. Neither is primary or fallback. Grok Bot is the voice-facing interface. The desks below are the running agents. Everything on this page runs on Jason’s development server, shown on test data only; it is the demonstration harness, not the proposed Milano Bro deployment.
